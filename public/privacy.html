Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026

Launchpad helps you build a resume, find jobs near you, and apply to them. This page says exactly what information that involves, where it goes, and what we never do with it.

What stays on your device

Your resumes, saved and tracked jobs, notes, settings, your home ZIP code, and the original wording you replaced with a "Polish" suggestion are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers. Clearing your browser data, or using Settings → Your data → Clear all data, removes them.

What our server handles, and does not keep

  • Job searches. The keywords, location or ZIP code, and filters you search with are sent to public job boards (for example Adzuna, USAJOBS, The Muse, Remotive and Google Jobs via JSearch) to fetch listings. Results are cached briefly (about 15 minutes) so repeated searches are fast. Searches are not tied to you and are not logged with any identifier.
  • Writing help. When you use Polish, AI drafts, tailoring or cover letters, the text involved (for example a resume bullet, your resume, or a job description) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to produce the suggestion, and the answer is returned to your browser. We do not store that text. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on it.
  • Talk instead of type. The microphone button uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Your browser vendor (for example Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari) may process the audio; Launchpad never receives audio.

Connecting your email (Gmail or Outlook)

If you choose to connect your email so Launchpad can send applications for you, we request only permission to send email on your behalf (Google scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send; Microsoft permission Mail.Send).

  • We use it only to send job applications that you reviewed and approved on the "check what goes out" screen. Nothing is sent without that screen.
  • We cannot read, search, delete or organize your email. We never access your inbox, contacts or calendar.
  • We store the sign-in token needed to send, encrypted, only while your email is connected. Disconnecting in Settings, or removing Launchpad from your Google or Microsoft account's connected apps, deletes it.
  • We keep a record of what we sent (to which employer, when, and which resume) so you can track your applications. You can delete it at any time.

Launchpad's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, transfer it to others except as needed to send the email you approved, or use it to train AI models. No person reads it unless you ask us to for support, or the law requires it.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your information.
  • Show you ads or share data with advertisers.
  • Apply to a job, or send anything, that you did not approve.

Cookies and analytics

Launchpad uses no advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party analytics.

Children

Launchpad is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes to how connected email is used will be shown in the app before they apply.
